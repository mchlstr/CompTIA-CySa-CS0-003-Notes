# WHOIS

## What it is
Protocol and service for querying domain registration information.

## Purpose
Retrieve domain ownership, registrar, and contact info for threat intelligence.

## Key Features
- Domain registration data  
- Administrative and technical contacts  
- Historical records (where available)

## Practical Use Cases
- OSINT investigations  
- Identify domain ownership of suspicious domains  
- Track threat actor infrastructure

## Tips
- Combine with DNS and IP intelligence  
- Privacy-protected domains may limit info
